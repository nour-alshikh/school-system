Under **My portal**:

- **My exams**: exams assigned to you. **Start** an open exam, **Continue** one in progress, and **View result** after submitting. An exam can only be taken between its opening and closing times.
- **My attendance**: your attendance record.
- **My subjects**: the subjects you study.
- **My fees**, **Library** and **My meetings** (online lessons) when your school uses them.
- The dashboard shows what's coming up and the school calendar.
