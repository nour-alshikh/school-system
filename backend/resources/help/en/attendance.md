**Academics → Attendance → Take attendance**: pick a classroom and a date, mark each student **Present, Absent, Late or Excused** (with an optional note), then **Save attendance**. Saving again for the same day updates it.

- Teachers see only the classrooms they teach; **My classrooms** shows which are marked today.
- Days outside the school days (Settings → School year) show a notice, but attendance can still be taken, e.g. for a make-up day.
- **Attendance history** shows past records for a classroom and date range.
- Students and parents see their own attendance in their portal; the admin dashboard shows today's rate and the last 30 days.
