**Academics → Academic years** lists the school's years. Add one with its start year (e.g. 2026 for 2026/2027) and tick **This is the current academic year** for the one in progress; only one is current.

Fees and student registration use academic years. The year menu in the top bar changes which year pages show. At the end of a year, move students on with **Promote students**.
