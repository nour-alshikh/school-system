**People → Students → Add student** opens a step-by-step form: student info (names in both languages, email, password, gender, nationality…), academic info (stage, grade, classroom, academic year), the parent account, father's and mother's details, optional attachments (images up to 1MB each), then confirm.

- **Siblings**: use the parent's existing email and leave the parent password blank; the child is added to that parent's account.
- **All students** can be searched and filtered by stage, grade, classroom and status (active / graduated). Open a student to see their details and, with the fees module, their charges.
- Selecting students from one classroom lets you promote or graduate them from the list.
- Deleting a student removes their record and their student and parent accounts; it can't be undone.
