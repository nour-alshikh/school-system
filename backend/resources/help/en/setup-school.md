A new school starts empty. The **Set up your school** checklist on the dashboard shows each step and links to where it's done. A good order:

1. **Settings → School year**: school days and currency. **Settings → Branding**: name, logo, colour.
2. **Academic years**: add the current year and mark it as current.
3. **Stages** (e.g. Primary), then **Grades** inside each stage, then **Classrooms** inside each grade.
4. **Subjects**: each is taught to whole stages or specific grades.
5. **Teachers**: add them, give them a login, and assign their subjects.
6. **Students**: register each student with the parent account.

Shortcut: on an empty school the checklist offers **Create a standard structure** (this year, 3 stages, 12 grades, a classroom per grade and 10 subjects). Rename or remove anything afterwards. The checklist can be hidden with its ✕ button.
