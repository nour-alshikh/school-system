title: AI features
feature: ai
keywords: ai, assistant, draft, translate, artificial intelligence, chatbot, privacy, history
---

- **✦ Draft with AI** on forms (exams, calendar events, meetings, library) turns rough notes into a filled-in draft. Nothing is saved until you press the form's own Save; **Undo AI draft** restores what was there.
- The **✦** button beside an empty Arabic or English name translates from the other language.
- **This assistant** (✦ in the top bar, or Ctrl/⌘ J) explains how to use the system. It can't see your school's data (students, attendance, fees) and can't change anything.
- Only what you type is sent to the AI provider. Chats are kept for 30 days; **New chat** starts over and **Clear history** deletes them.
- An admin can turn AI off in **Settings → General**.
