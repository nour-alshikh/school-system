- **✦ Draft with AI** on forms (exams, calendar events, meetings, library) turns rough notes into a filled-in draft. Nothing is saved until you press the form's own Save; **Undo AI draft** restores what was there.
- The **✦** button beside an empty Arabic or English name translates from the other language.
- **This assistant** (✦ in the top bar, or Ctrl/⌘ J) explains how to use the system. It never changes anything.
- When an admin turns on **Let the assistant read school data** (Settings → General), it can also answer questions like "who was absent today?", "who still owes fees?", "how did 9-B do on the last quiz?" or "how is my child doing?" — only with what that person is allowed to see on their own pages. It's off by default, because the results of those lookups are sent to the AI provider.
- Otherwise only what you type is sent. Chats are kept for 30 days; **New chat** starts over and **Clear history** deletes them.
- An admin can turn AI off entirely in **Settings → General**.
