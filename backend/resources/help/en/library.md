The **Library** holds grade-level resources tagged by subject: links, or files (PDF, document, image or video, up to 1MB).

Staff and teachers add one with **New resource**: title, optional description, grade, subject tags, and a link or file. Teachers can pick only grades they teach. Students and parents see the resources for the student's grade in their portal's **Library**, filtered by subject.
