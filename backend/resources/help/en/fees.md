**Finance → Fees** defines what the school charges: a name in both languages, the academic year, the amount, and who it applies to (all students, a stage or a grade).

- **Charge all matching students** creates a charge for every matching student who hasn't been charged that fee yet.
- On a student's page, **Fee charges** lists each charge with due, paid, waived and balance. From there: **Add charge**, **Edit amount**, **Record payment** (amount, date, method: cash, bank transfer, card, other; a negative amount records a refund), **Waive**, and **Print** the payment history.
- The currency is set in **Settings → School year**. Parents and students see their charges under **My fees**; the dashboard shows what's outstanding and collected.
