The school is organised as **stages → grades → classrooms**.

- **Academics → Stages**: e.g. Kindergarten, Primary, Preparatory. Each has an English and an Arabic name.
- **Academics → Grades**: each grade belongs to a stage (e.g. Grade 1 in Primary). Filter the list by stage.
- Names are entered in both languages; the ✦ button beside an empty language field can translate from the other one when AI is on.
- A stage or grade that still has grades, classrooms or students under it may not be deletable; move or remove those first.
