Under **My portal**:

- **My info**: your name, email and password, and your child's details; you can update the guardian details there.
- **My attendance**: your child's attendance record.
- **My fees** and **Library** when your school uses them.
- The dashboard shows your child's upcoming events, lessons and exams.

With more than one child at the school, all of them are on the same account; switch between them where the page offers it.
