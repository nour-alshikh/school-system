**Academics → Exams → Create exam**: title, type, subject, and who takes it (a whole grade or one classroom; teachers pick one of their classroom subjects). Optional instructions, a duration in minutes, and opening / closing times.

- **Questions** are multiple choice: question text, marks, options, and the correct option selected. **Add question** for more.
- **Status**: a draft is hidden from students; **Published** makes it appear under students' **My exams** while it's open.
- **✦ Draft with AI** adds questions from a request or a pasted lesson (after yours); check every answer before publishing.
- **View results** lists each student's status and score. Students see their result after submitting.
