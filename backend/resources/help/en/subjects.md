**Academics → Subjects** lists what the school teaches. Each subject has English and Arabic names and is **taught to** either whole stages or specific grades.

After adding subjects: assign them to teachers (**Teachers → Subject assignments**), then pick each classroom's teacher per subject in **Classrooms → Subjects & teachers**. Exams and library resources are tagged by subject, and students see theirs under **My subjects**.
