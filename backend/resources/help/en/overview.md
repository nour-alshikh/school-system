---
title: Using the app
keywords: help, start, menu, sidebar, search, language, theme
---

The sidebar lists only the pages you can open. On a phone, open it with the menu button at the top.

- **Search** (Ctrl K, or ⌘ K on a Mac) jumps to any page; staff who manage students can also find a student by name.
- The top bar switches the language (English / العربية), the theme (light / dark) and the academic year being shown.
- Your picture in the top bar opens your account menu: change your photo, switch to another school or role you belong to, create a new school, or log out.
- Each person sees what their role allows: admins manage the school, teachers their classrooms, students and parents their own portal.
- Some modules (fees, exams, meetings, library, AI) depend on the school's plan; if one is missing, it isn't included in the plan.
