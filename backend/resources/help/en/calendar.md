The calendar is on the dashboard. Everyone sees school events; students, parents and teachers also see a **Coming up** list with events, online lessons and exams closing soon.

Staff with calendar access add an event with **Add event** (title, date, description), and can edit, delete, or move it to another day. **✦ Draft with AI** fills the title, description and date from a sentence like "sports day next Thursday".
