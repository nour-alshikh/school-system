**Promote students** moves a classroom's students into a new classroom, grade or stage: pick the classroom, then where they're going, uncheck anyone staying, and confirm. **Promotion history** can roll a promotion back, but only while it's still the student's most recent change.

**Graduate students** takes a classroom's students out of the active roster (e.g. the final grade): pick the classroom, uncheck anyone not graduating, confirm. **Graduation history** can roll it back while they're still graduated.

Both can also start from **All students** by selecting students from one classroom.
