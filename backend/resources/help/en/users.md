**People → Manage users** lists everyone with an account at the school and their roles. Accounts are created where the person is added: teachers get a login from the teacher form, students and parents from the student registration form.

Roles: **admin** (manages the school), **teacher**, **student**, **parent**. One person can belong to several schools or roles and switch between them from the account menu (their picture in the top bar).
