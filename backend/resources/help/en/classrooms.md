**Academics → Classrooms** lists the classrooms (sections) of each grade, e.g. 1A and 1B.

- **Add** creates several at once: pick the grade, type the names (e.g. 1A), optionally a capacity, and **Add another classroom** for more rows.
- In each classroom, **Subjects & teachers** sets which teacher teaches each of that grade's subjects. Only teachers assigned to that subject (Teachers → Subject assignments) can be picked.
- Teachers see the classrooms they teach under **My classrooms** and can take their attendance.
- You need a stage and a grade before you can add classrooms.
