**People → Teachers** lists the teaching staff with their status (active, inactive, on leave).

- **Add / edit**: name, email, phone, joining date, status. Under **Login access**, set a password to let the teacher log in; leave it blank later to keep it unchanged.
- **Subject assignments** chooses which subjects each teacher teaches. Then, in each classroom, pick the teacher for each subject.
- With a login, teachers see My classrooms, take attendance, create exams for their classrooms, schedule meetings and add library resources (depending on the plan).
