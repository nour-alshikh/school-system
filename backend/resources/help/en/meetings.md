**Communication → Meetings** schedules Zoom lessons. An admin first connects the school's Zoom account with **Connect Zoom** on that page; until then, teachers are told to ask an admin.

**New meeting**: title, optional description, one or more classrooms, start time and duration. Students in those classrooms see it under **My meetings** and on their dashboard; **Start meeting** opens it in Zoom. Cancelling a meeting removes it from Zoom and from every student's list.
